What is an Environmental Chemist?

An Environmental Chemist is a scientist who protects our planet! They study soil, air, and water to find toxins and use nature and science to clean them up.

🌊 Water Testing

Testing rivers and oceans to make water safe for animals and humans.

🌱 Soil Healing

Using special plants to naturally absorb toxins from polluted ground.

💨 Clean Air

Measuring air quality and helping factories reduce chemical waste.
